## What you get

- A full-panel **pixel-art office** where every thread is a little worker. Where it is tells you what it's doing: busy threads at their desks, planners at the whiteboard, threads that need you in the waiting room, idle ones in the kitchen or lounge. One click puts a name tag over everyone, solid for unread and outlined for read.
- **Office life**: idle agents get together for coffee, a hallway chat, ping-pong or darts, with comic speech bubbles about tech news, small talk or what their thread actually did. They have lunch together after noon (a delivery brings pizza, sushi, burgers, tacos or ramen; some bring a lunchbox). Someone occasionally gets a (harmless) shock in the server room, and a technician comes round to fix things, up a ladder if need be.
- **Plans for your review** are presented at the whiteboard in the glass meeting room. When you're writing a prompt for a thread, it sits in your office going over documents with you.
- **Thirteen sceneries**, one click away: the classic office, neon city streets, tents among the redwoods, a surf villa, a New York penthouse, a dusty forgotten annex, a school, a startup house, a mountain cabin, a villain's lair in a volcano, a spaceship, a coffee shop, and a home office where everyone works from home and meets on video calls. Each with its own floor plan, pets, visitors and things to click; same people and events, different world.
- **A short tour** the first time you open it, one thing at a time; take it again from the sticky-note legend.
- Desks furnished by **model** (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku, GPT/Codex) with flat screens, the odd retro CRT and a Vision Pro or two, chairs by provider, and a shirt badge for what the thread is about.
- The office **remembers what you last saw**: coming back, only threads whose state changed get up and walk.
- A **home screen on the wall**: every enabled provider's usage on a pinboard, a calendar, a status light board, and a big TV with office news and live head counts, the office weather, tech headlines (Hacker News, Lobsters, Techmeme, The Verge, Ars Technica, DEV, Slashdot, the GitHub Blog, a mix, or any RSS or Atom feed) and, if you use the Work Dashboard plugin, your inbox.
- **Life in the office**: Biscuit the dog, Pixel the cat, a roomba, fish, a cleaning crew in the evening, a night guard, a glowing server room with an AI cluster, day and night with your clock, and your **real local weather** outside the windows.
- **Office events** for idle threads (parties, cake, karaoke, cornhole, darts, kids day…) and **festivals** through the year with decorations and their own party: snow day, Lunar New Year, Valentine's Day, Carnival, St. Patrick's Day, Holi, Easter, Maifest, summer, Oktoberfest, Halloween, Diwali, Laternenfest, Thanksgiving and the holidays.
- Lots to **click**, with synthesized sounds: the espresso machine, vending machine, piano, dartboard, plants, the globe, the couch, your own desk (whistles for the dog), and the windows (birds, crickets, rain, a foghorn…). Sound, scenery and the buddy live behind the gear at the top right. And every scenery hides a few easter eggs of its own for you to find.
- An **office buddy** that strolls in a corner of bb wherever you are (inside a thread, as that thread's figure), counting the threads with news since you last looked; the rail icon shows the same count.
- Fits any panel, including a phone held upright, and stays light on your computer: it pauses when the panel is off screen.

## Good to know

- Outside requests: the tech news source you picked for the TECH channel (at most every 20 minutes; `techNews false` turns it off), and the weather from Open-Meteo for your approximate location from your IP via geojs.io, or a place you set (`weather false` turns it off).
- Usage notes come from plugins that publish provider usage (most provider plugins do).
- Lunar New Year, Diwali and Holi dates are looked up through 2030.
- Furniture sprites from Pixel Agents (MIT).
