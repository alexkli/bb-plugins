## What you get

- A full-panel **pixel-art office** where every thread is a little worker. Where it is tells you what it's doing: busy threads at their desks, planners at the whiteboard, threads that need you in the waiting room with a title tag over their heads, idle ones in the kitchen or lounge.
- **Plans for your review** are presented at the whiteboard in the glass meeting room. When you're writing a prompt for a thread, it sits in your office going over documents with you.
- Desks furnished by **model** (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku, GPT/Codex), chairs by provider, and a shirt badge for what the thread is about.
- A **home screen on the wall**: every enabled provider's usage on a pinboard, a calendar, a status light board, and a big TV with office news, Hacker News headlines and, if you use the Work Dashboard plugin, your inbox.
- **Life in the office**: Biscuit the dog, Pixel the cat, a roomba, fish, a cleaning crew in the evening, a night guard, day and night with your clock, rainy days.
- **Office events** for idle threads (parties, cake, karaoke, cornhole, darts, kids day…) and **festivals** through the year with decorations and their own party: snow day, Lunar New Year, Valentine's Day, Carnival, St. Patrick's Day, Holi, Easter, Maifest, summer, Oktoberfest, Halloween, Diwali, Laternenfest, Thanksgiving and the holidays.
- Lots to **click**, with synthesized sounds: the espresso machine, vending machine, piano, dartboard, plants, the globe, your own desk (whistles for the dog). Sound controls live on the office speakers.
- An **office buddy** that strolls in a corner of bb wherever you are; click it to open the office.
- Fits any panel, including a phone held upright.

## Good to know

- The only outside request is to the Hacker News API for the TECH channel, at most every 20 minutes. Turn it off with `bb plugin config cozy-hq set techNews false`.
- Usage notes come from plugins that publish provider usage (most provider plugins do).
- Lunar New Year, Diwali and Holi dates are looked up through 2030.
- Furniture sprites from Pixel Agents (MIT).
